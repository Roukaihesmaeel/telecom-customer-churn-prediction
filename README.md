# Customer Churn Prediction

## Overview

A Machine Learning project designed to predict customer churn in the telecommunications industry.

The project focuses on identifying customers who may be at risk of leaving and providing insights that can support customer retention decisions.

## Business Objective

Customer churn can have a direct impact on revenue and customer lifetime value.

The goal of this project is to help businesses identify high-risk customers early and take proactive retention actions.

## Key Insights

The analysis identified several patterns associated with customer churn:

* Month-to-month customers showed a substantially higher churn rate than customers with longer-term contracts.
* Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to leave.
* Higher monthly charges were generally associated with higher churn rates.
* Contract type, internet service, payment method, online security, and technical support were among the important factors associated with churn.

## Model Performance

Several Machine Learning approaches were evaluated during the project.

The final approach focused on improving the detection of customers who are likely to churn.

Selected performance metrics:

* Accuracy: 73%
* Churn Recall: 80%
* Churn F1-Score: 61%

Recall was considered particularly important because identifying potential churners can help businesses take preventive action.

## Prediction Application

The project includes an interactive prediction interface that provides:

* Churn prediction
* Churn probability
* Customer risk level
* Business recommendation

Example:

**Prediction:** Yes
**Churn Probability:** 88.62%
**Risk Level:** High

The system can support customer retention teams by highlighting customers who may require additional attention.
